Paired robust-solver regression comparison

This run evaluates all 100 distinct hash-verified mono waveforms cached for
Rachel's historical Hugging Face benchmark. They are originally 0.5-second,
16 kHz sources. scipy.signal.resample_poly upsamples each by 3 to 48 kHz;
the central 2048 source samples are then selected before simulated propagation.
Upsampling adds no native 48 kHz source bandwidth. These 42.67 ms synthetic
windows match the hardware FFT length, not an actual hardware recording or
a replay of a continuous streamed recording.

Each source has one seeded full-circle azimuth at elevations 0, 30, 60 and
80 degrees, plus the 90-degree zenith case, at nominal SNR 30, 20, 10 and
0 dB. There are 2,000 intact comparisons and 16,000 damaged-array comparisons:
each of B/L/R/T is separately silent and separately noise-only for every
intact condition. The noise-only channel is its original baseline noisy signal
minus the identical clean propagated source, retaining the exact added noise
draw. The three healthy channels remain bit-for-bit unchanged. SNR labels
describe the intact baseline; there is no drone signal in a failed channel.

The same sources and fault cases were used during development. They exposed
an absolute GCC-peak confidence omission: weighting a fit by uniformly weak
peaks did not, by itself, reduce confidence. The final algorithm explicitly
includes absolute peak support in its confidence. This complete final rerun is
a regression comparison, not held-out performance validation. No numerical
threshold tuning occurred, but algorithm fixes were informed by test findings.
Confidence >= 0.2 remains an uncalibrated demo gate, and the robust confidence
score has a different formula from the original score.

The new variant withholds a unique direction in all 16,000 failed-microphone
cases: 13,782 ambiguous results and 2,218 unreliable results. The original
accepted 20 noise-only failures, including 10 with more than 15 degrees of
3D error; it already withheld all silent failures. The robust result therefore
improves noise-only rejection in this sweep. Withholding does not recover a
unique drone direction from the remaining microphones. Each fault mode has
6,891 candidate sets; 6,418 contain a candidate within 15 degrees of the known
direction. This is diagnostic set coverage, not unique localization accuracy.
No hemisphere assumption or known direction was supplied to the estimator.

This behavior has an intact-array coverage cost. At the fixed gate, the
original accepts 1,461/2,000 intact cases (73.05%); the robust variant accepts
1,261/2,000 (63.05%). Neither has an accepted error over 15 degrees in the
final comparison. By nominal SNR 30/20/10/0 dB, robust acceptance is
100%/99.6%/52.6%/0%, versus 100%/100%/92.2%/0% originally. Counting all
intact cases, including withheld cases as failures, raw vectors within
15 degrees decrease from 1,890/2,000 to 1,805/2,000; defined output decreases
from 1,999 to 1,852. Lower conditional error statistics must be read alongside
this reduced coverage.

The confidence correction leaves every raw vector, candidate, status, fit
diagnostic and original comparison value identical to the initial complete
development run; confidence and its gate decisions change. AUDIT.json records
the independent per-row and aggregate comparison. Current solver and benchmark
hashes match provenance, and all reused original result-file hashes match.
The original sig_proc_tri.py was not modified.

These are simulated synchronized plane-wave arrivals with independent added
white noise. Existing source recording noise propagates coherently and is
removed with the failed-channel source. Whole-waveform white-noise SNR has a
different in-band noise level at 16 and 48 kHz; comparisons must not imply a
causal sample-rate advantage. Repeated cases are not independent recordings.
No helmet model, real microphone response, head shadow, diffuse noise,
reverberation, wind, or native 48 kHz source recordings are validated. These
results concern the separate standalone robust solver, not the live/firmware
bearing path, and do not establish failure-free performance on new recordings.
