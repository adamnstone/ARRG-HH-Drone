Separate robust solver verification

Implementation: sig_proc_tri_robust.py, copied from the original sig_proc_tri.py.
The original solver remains byte-for-byte unchanged. No live/firmware bearing
implementation was replaced by this experiment.

Changes: bias-corrected multi-segment spectral coherence; quality-weighted Huber
delay fitting; effective weighted rank/conditioning checks; unit-direction
consistency checks; explicit rank-2 mirror candidates; optional caller-supplied
hemisphere assumption; status/reason and per-pair/microphone diagnostics.
Absolute GCC peak quality caps confidence so weak peaks cannot be concealed by
the scale invariance of weighted fitting.

Final automated suite:
python3 -m pytest -q
74 passed, 10 skipped, 14 warnings in 14.46 seconds.
The 10 skipped classifier-assisted tests require TensorFlow or LiteRT, neither
installed in this test environment. Warnings are existing plotting-library
deprecations. All 22 new robust-solver regression tests passed.

New tests cover independent analytic arrival signals at 16/48 kHz, signed and
vertical directions, arbitrary rotated/translated geometry and channel order,
all four dead-mic mirror cases, zero-projection overhead/front cases, explicit
hemisphere selection and contradictions, coplanar/near-coplanar arrays, silence,
two surviving mics, independent noise, weak noisy channels, gain/DC invariance,
periodic aliases, tuple wrappers, absolute-quality rescaling, and a natural
low-SNR signal that previously produced false confidence.

The full 100-clip comparison has 18,000 cases. All 16,000 missing-source faults
withhold a unique direction; 13,782 return ambiguous candidates and 2,218 are
unreliable. Intact-array confidence-gated coverage is 1,261/2,000 (63.05%) versus
1,461/2,000 (73.05%) in the original; all accepted vectors are within 15 degrees.
This is improved fault withholding with reduced coverage, not a claim of better
raw accuracy. Coverage, original source hashes, both solver hashes, matching
saved original results, vectors/candidates/errors/gates, and all 83 aggregate
groups passed an independent audit (AUDIT.json).

The partial-source-attenuation supplement has 1,700 paired cases at nominal
20 dB intact-array SNR (see ../results_hf_robust_attenuation_2048). At -30 dB
attenuation of one source-bearing channel the robust version accepts 6/400
cases with no error above 15 degrees, versus original 72/400 including 2 such
errors. Other attenuations and intact controls are reported separately.

These are development-informed regression tests with mono sources and simulated
synchronized microphone signals, not held-out or mounted-helmet validation.
No unique direction is credited for merely having a candidate near the truth.
Neither solver receives true directions or automatic hemisphere assumptions.
The confidence threshold is heuristic; zero observed fault acceptances is not
a microphone-health guarantee. Coherent reflections can still appear reliable.
Actual coordinates, mounting, head orientation and helmet acoustic responses
need real recordings; the current free-field model does not correct scattering.
