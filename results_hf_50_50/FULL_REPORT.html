Four-microphone direction estimation

Full comparison of the original and robust solvers: 50 intact + 50 degraded Hugging Face source recordings, with paired sensitivity checks.

The primary comparison contains 100 inputs at a nominal intact-channel SNR of 20 dB. The complete sweep contains 1,200 paired scenarios and 2,400 estimator calls using 100 distinct cached recordings. The primary 100 are a subset of this sweep, not an additional 100 recordings.

Across the primary 100 cases, Original accepts 52 and withholds 48; Robust accepts 50 and withholds 50. Accepted errors above 15°: Original 0, Robust 0. These are measured outcomes on this selected simulation protocol, not a calibrated failure probability.

Both solvers have zero incorrectly accepted directions in the primary comparison. This primary sample therefore does not demonstrate a safety advantage for either solver. The robust version withholds more degraded inputs; the paired sensitivity section quantifies that coverage cost.

What was tested and what the numbers mean

The source groups are disjoint within this run: 50 recordings supply the intact group and 50 different recordings supply the degraded group. All 100 clips were already used in earlier algorithm development; this is stress validation on previously seen recordings, not a held-out evaluation.

Real mono drone recordings were resampled from 16 kHz to 48 kHz using 3:1 polyphase resampling, then cropped to the central 2,048 samples (42.67 ms) before simulated propagation. This is not native 48 kHz multi-microphone audio. Four synchronized free-field plane-wave channels were synthesized for the B/L/R/T geometry, with independent added white noise.

Each group has 10 recordings at each elevation 0°, 30°, 60°, 80°, 90°. Azimuths cover the full circle using the frozen seeded assignment; azimuth is undefined at exactly overhead, but full 3D angular error remains valid. Each degraded input affects one selected microphone: B 13 cases, L 13, R 12, T 12.

Limits and interpretation

The degraded models change only the source amplitude of one channel while keeping its original added noise. They do not model a helmet transfer function, head shadow with frequency-dependent phase, microphone timing offsets, wind, reflections, motion or clipping. No synchronized recording from the physical four-microphone headset is evaluated here.

The 0.2 confidence gate is a demo setting and is not a calibrated probability. These selected counts do not establish a future failure rate. Conservative withholding trades coverage for fewer actionable errors; it does not by itself improve raw angular accuracy. A three-microphone mirror pair is ambiguity, not a measured unique 3D location.

B/L/R/T geometry (meters; x right, y forward, z up): [[0.0, -0.1, 0.0], [-0.09, 0.0, 0.0], [0.09, 0.0, 0.0], [0.0, 0.0, 0.09]]. Measure the hardware positions and verify channel order before deployment.
